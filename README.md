# Green Haven Foods — Digital Supply Chain Management Project

## Project Overview
This capstone project examines supply-chain challenges at **Green Haven Foods**, an FMCG business handling perishable snack products. It reviews sales and inventory patterns and proposes digital improvements to support demand planning, inventory control, delivery visibility, and sustainability.

## Business Problems Covered
- Inaccurate demand forecasting
- Inventory shortages and stockout incidents
- Delivery delays and freshness risks
- Manual warehouse and procurement processes
- Product waste and inefficient logistics planning

## Analysis Included
- SKU-wise sales and average monthly demand
- Inventory and stockout analysis
- Demand–supply gap examples
- Basic average-based demand forecasting
- Root-cause analysis and recommendations

## Proposed Digital Solutions
- **AI / forecasting:** demand prediction and transportation planning
- **ERP and digital procurement:** purchasing workflows and supplier coordination
- **Smart warehouse / IoT:** barcode or RFID inventory tracking, GPS delivery tracking, and environmental monitoring
- **Blockchain:** supply-chain traceability, compliance records, and smart-contract concepts
- **Sustainability:** discussion of resource use, waste reduction, and Life Cycle Assessment concepts
